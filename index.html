The current system

People who move to the Netherlands permanently sometimes have to complete civic integration (inburgering). They learn Dutch, learn about Dutch society and participate through work, school or volunteering.

Most participants have three years to finish the process. The municipality helps them choose the right pathway.

B1 pathway

Language learning combined with work or participation.

Education pathway

Primarily for young people preparing for further education.

Z pathway

For people who need more help becoming independent.

What is the KNM exam?

KNM stands for Kennis van de Nederlandse Maatschappij. It tests what participants know about Dutch society. The exam is completed on a computer and takes 45 minutes.
